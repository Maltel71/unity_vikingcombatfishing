## About This Game

```
ABOUT THE GAME

Step into the boots of Ragnar, a Viking with two problems: he is hungry, and the woods are full of gnomes.

Ragnar Goes Fishing is a 2D arcade game that puts a fishing rod in one hand and an axe in the other. Every fish you land keeps you alive. Every gnome you cut down buys you time to cast again. Stand your ground long enough and your name goes up in the Halls of Valhalla.


FISH TO SURVIVE

Health does not regenerate. Fish is the only thing standing between Ragnar and the afterlife.

Cast into the lake and wait for the bite, then reel it in before it shakes loose. Perch, roach, flounder, pike, salmon and rainbow trout each restore a different amount of health and are worth different points. The rarer the catch, the harder the fight to land it.

Leave a fish lying on the bank too long and it turns. Flies find it, and eating it costs you health instead of restoring it. Sometimes the lake gives you an old boot, which is worth nothing at all — but it does make Ragnar furious.


BATTLE NORDIC FOLKLORE

You will not fish in peace. Gnomes come in waves, more of them each time, and they do not care that you are mid-cast.

Every few waves something worse arrives:

• The Troll — slow, enormous, and hits like a falling tree
• The Gnome Berserker — fast, relentless, and swings before you can back off
• Nacken — a drowned hand that rises from the lake and reaches for you, with gnomes sprinting in while you deal with it

Beat them and they come back stronger. There is no last wave.


BUILD THE RAGE

Every clean hit fills the rage meter. Take a hit and you lose some of it. Fill it completely and unleash the berserk swing — three times the damage, wide enough to clear a crowd, and enough to send a troll staggering backwards.


KEY FEATURES

• Two jobs at once — reel with one hand, fight with the other, and learn when to drop the rod
• Fish that fight back — six species with their own bite times, reel difficulty and rewards
• Endless waves — the gnomes keep coming and keep multiplying
• Three bosses from Nordic folklore — each returning stronger every time you kill it
• The berserk swing — earned through clean fighting, spent in one devastating blow
• Halls of Valhalla — a global leaderboard where your catch and your kill count decide your place
• 10 achievements — from your first fish to clearing 250 gnomes


Fish. Fight. Die well.
```
